# Classic Theme Audit

## Style Direction

Classic should feel warm, editorial, crafted, and established. It is still premium and modern, but the mood should lean towards bronze glass, old-world polish, and calm confidence.

## Palette

- Base: espresso, dark walnut, and warm black.
- Accent: bronze, parchment, muted gold, and soft amber.
- Avoid: futuristic cyan dominance, harsh neon, or purple-led gradients.
- Text: ivory headings with warm beige supporting text.

## Assets

- Home atmosphere: `/images/themes/classic-atmosphere.svg`.
- Portfolio feature atmosphere: `/images/themes/classic-atmosphere.svg`.
- Brand mark: crystal UFO logo, warmed through media/theme filters.
- WebGL crystal: champagne, bronze, amber, and ivory facets.

## Navbar Rules

- Navbar should look like warm smoked glass, not a sci-fi blue panel.
- UFO beam may remain as a signature effect, but its colour should read amber/bronze.
- Active links should glow softly with gold warmth.
- CTA should feel like a polished dark bronze control.

## Audit Checklist

- No hardcoded cyan/purple dominates navbar surfaces.
- Theme picker uses warm borders and label colours.
- Mobile menu keeps rounded, warm-glass treatment.
- Portfolio and hero imagery use classic atmosphere assets.
- Hover and focus states remain accessible against warm dark surfaces.
