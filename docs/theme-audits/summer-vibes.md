# Summer Vibes Theme Audit

## Style Direction

Summer vibes should feel warm, sunny, relaxed, and premium. It should be bright and inviting without becoming playful to the point of feeling less professional.

## Palette

- Base: warm cream, peach, and pale sand surfaces.
- Accent: orange, golden yellow, and small aqua highlights.
- Avoid: cold futuristic glow, heavy black panels, or purple dominance.
- Text: warm brown/ink with clear contrast.

## Assets

- Home atmosphere: `/images/themes/summer-vibes-atmosphere.svg`.
- Portfolio feature atmosphere: `/images/themes/summer-vibes-atmosphere.svg`.
- Brand mark: crystal UFO logo, warmed through media/theme filters.
- WebGL crystal: cream, gold, orange, aqua, and white facets.

## Navbar Rules

- Navbar should feel like warm frosted glass over sunlight.
- UFO effect should become golden/orange with subtle aqua support.
- Active links should glow with warm yellow/orange energy.
- CTA should remain clear and premium, not beachy or novelty.

## Audit Checklist

- Navbar shell, mobile menu, active links, and CTA all shift warm.
- Theme picker labels and borders use orange/gold accents.
- Hero/media assets use Summer vibes atmosphere artwork.
- WebGL crystal facets match summer lighting.
- Focus and hover states remain accessible on light warm surfaces.
