# Fresh Theme Audit

## Style Direction

Fresh should feel calm, organic, modern, and optimistic. It should retain the premium structure of Crystal Powers while shifting the visual energy towards mint, teal, and growth.

## Palette

- Base: soft mint, pale green, and clean teal surfaces.
- Accent: teal, emerald, and aqua.
- Avoid: purple-led glow, heavy navy dominance, or overly clinical blue.
- Text: deep green/teal ink with bright surfaces.

## Assets

- Home atmosphere: `/images/themes/fresh-atmosphere.svg`.
- Portfolio feature atmosphere: `/images/themes/fresh-atmosphere.svg`.
- Brand mark: crystal UFO logo, treated through fresh media filters.
- WebGL crystal: mint, teal, emerald, and white facets.

## Navbar Rules

- Navbar should feel like fresh frosted glass with teal energy.
- UFO beam can stay, but should look mint/teal rather than electric blue.
- Active links should have a clean teal glow.
- CTA should be strong enough for contrast while still matching the fresh palette.

## Audit Checklist

- Navbar surfaces, active states, CTA, and mobile menu use teal/green tokens.
- Hero and portfolio assets use Fresh atmosphere artwork.
- WebGL crystal shifts away from blue-purple.
- Hover states feel lively but restrained.
- Mobile navbar maintains spacing and readability.
