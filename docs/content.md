## Brand Identity

**Studio name:** Crystal Powers  
**Positioning:** Premium web studio — polished websites, launch systems, and digital surfaces built for first impressions.  
**Target audience:** Businesses, freelancers, and founders who need a high-quality digital presence that converts.  
**Tone:** Direct, confident, premium without being opaque. No filler, no buzzword soup. Every sentence earns its place.

### Brand voice principles

| Principle | In practice |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Direct** | Lead with the outcome, not the process. Say "We organise the whole experience around clarity, trust, and action" not "We use an agile, human-centred approach." |
| **Controlled confidence** | State what the studio does well. Do not hedge with "we try to" or "we aim to". |
| **Premium through restraint** | Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No hyperbole. No em-dashes in headlines. |
| **Practical** | Acknowledge real problems ("a contact route that does not feel like an afterthought") rather than speaking in abstractions. |
| **No jargon** | "Cleaner navigation" not "UX optimisation". "Convincing proof" not "social proof integration". |

## Content Strategy Notes

- **Scan first.** Every section should communicate its core point from the heading alone.
- **Outcomes over features.** Describe what the client gets, not what Crystal Powers does.
- **Precision over length.** A three-word eyebrow beats a five-word one.
- **No promises that can't be kept.** Avoid superlatives like "best" or "fastest" without evidence.
- **Consistent terminology.** Use "brief" not "enquiry". Use "build" not "project" or "website". Use "surface" for any digital touchpoint.
