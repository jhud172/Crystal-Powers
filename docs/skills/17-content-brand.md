# 17 - Content And Brand

## Purpose

Use for public copy, headings, CTAs, legal wording, documentation that may influence public copy, and brand consistency.

## Rules

- Public brand: `Crystal Powers`.
- Use British English.
- Tone: premium, direct, clear, restrained, practical.
- Do not use old public-facing brand names.
- Do not make unrealistic monopoly claims, exaggerated guarantees, invented outcomes, invented testimonials, or invented analytics.
- Avoid vague generic agency copy.
- Focus on websites, digital systems, community platforms, automation, creative support, structured delivery, and ongoing support.
- Preserve approved content unless the task requests a rewrite.

## Legal Wording

Only where genuinely required: `Crystal Powers is operated by Crystal Production LTD.`

## Validation

Search changed public-facing files for old branding and American spellings unless code/API terms require them.
